Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how MHRAS collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal information and protected health information (PHI) in connection with the Mental Health Risk Assessment System. It is informational and does not constitute a representation that MHRAS is HIPAA compliant or that any particular privacy law applies to a specific use.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address (from Firebase Authentication)
  • Display name (if provided via Google SSO)
  • Profile photo URL (if provided via Google SSO)
  • Authentication provider (email/password or Google)
  • System-assigned user ID
  • Role assignment (user, reviewer, admin)
  • Account creation timestamp

1.2 Patient Screening Data

When clinical users process patient screenings, we collect:

  • Anonymized patient identifiers (user-provided)
  • PHQ-9 depression screening scores
  • GAD-7 anxiety screening scores
  • Wearable biometric data (heart rate, sleep patterns)
  • Electronic medical record data (diagnosis codes, medications)
  • Risk assessment outputs (scores, levels, contributing factors)
  • Clinical explanations and interpretations
  • Screening timestamps

1.3 Clinical Review Data

When reviewers process flagged cases, we collect:

  • Review status (pending, approved, escalated, closed)
  • Reviewer assignments
  • Clinical notes and annotations
  • Review timestamps and audit trail

1.4 Technical Data

We automatically collect:

  • IP addresses (for rate limiting and security)
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system information
  • Request timestamps and response times
  • API usage metrics (anonymized, aggregated)
  • Error logs and system performance data

2. How We Use Your Information

2.1 Service Provision

We use collected information to:

  • Authenticate users and manage access control
  • Process screening data and generate risk assessments
  • Facilitate clinical review workflows
  • Provide clinical decision-support outputs
  • Maintain audit trails for regulatory compliance

2.2 System Improvement

We use de-identified, aggregated data to:

  • Improve the accuracy of clinical risk models
  • Enhance system performance and reliability
  • Develop new features and clinical capabilities
  • Conduct quality assurance and safety monitoring

2.3 Legal and Compliance

We use information as necessary to:

  • Comply with HIPAA, HITECH, and applicable health privacy laws
  • Respond to lawful requests from regulatory authorities
  • Enforce our Terms of Service
  • Protect against fraud, unauthorized access, and other harms
  • Maintain records as required by applicable law

3. Protected Health Information (PHI)

MHRAS processes Protected Health Information (PHI) as defined by HIPAA. All use and disclosure of PHI is governed by our Business Associate Agreement (BAA) and the HIPAA Privacy Rule (45 CFR Part 160 and Part 164).

3.1 PHI Handling

All patient screening data is treated as PHI and is:

  • Encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256)
  • Stored in HIPAA-compliant Google Cloud Firestore
  • Accessed only by authorized users with appropriate role assignments
  • Subject to minimum necessary access controls
  • Retained only for the legally required minimum period

3.2 De-identification

Patient data entered into MHRAS is anonymized using a cryptographic salt-based hashing mechanism. De-identified data used for model improvement complies with the HIPAA Safe Harbor method (45 CFR §164.514(b)(2)) and does not constitute PHI.

3.3 Breach Notification

In the event of a breach of unsecured PHI, we will notify affected individuals and the Department of Health and Human Services (HHS) in accordance with the HIPAA Breach Notification Rule (45 CFR §§164.400-414). Notifications will be provided without unreasonable delay and no later than 60 days following discovery of the breach.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

4.1 We Do Not Sell Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information or PHI to any third party for marketing or any other purpose.

4.2 Service Providers

We share data with the following service providers under Business Associate Agreements where required:

Provider Service Data Processed Location
Google Cloud (Firebase) Authentication, Database Account data, screening data, PHI United States
Vercel Hosting, Serverless Functions API requests, session data United States

4.3 Legal Disclosures

We may disclose information when required by law, including:

  • In response to valid subpoenas, court orders, or warrants
  • To comply with HIPAA-required disclosures to HHS
  • To report suspected child abuse or neglect
  • To prevent or lessen a serious threat to health or safety
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets (with prior notice)

5. Your Rights

5.1 HIPAA Rights

If you are a patient whose data has been processed through MHRAS, you have the following rights under HIPAA:

  • Right to Access: Request copies of your health information
  • Right to Amendment: Request corrections to your health information
  • Right to Accounting: Request a list of disclosures of your PHI
  • Right to Restrictions: Request restrictions on uses and disclosures of your PHI
  • Right to Confidential Communications: Request communications through alternative means
  • Right to Notice: Receive notice of our privacy practices

5.2 GDPR Rights (EU Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have additional rights under GDPR:

  • Right to Rectification: Correct inaccurate personal data
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of personal data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Limit how we use your data
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Right to Object: Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent at any time
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: File a complaint with your local supervisory authority

5.3 CCPA Rights (California Residents)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act:

  • Right to Know: Request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information collected
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of personal information
  • Right to Opt-Out: Opt out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell data)
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: Not be discriminated against for exercising your rights

5.4 Exercising Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, use the privacy contact published by the organization operating this deployment. We will respond within the period required by applicable law and may require identity verification before processing your request.

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption: All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256)
  • Access Control: Role-based access control with minimum necessary principle
  • Authentication: Firebase Authentication with email/password and Google SSO
  • Rate Limiting: API rate limiting to prevent abuse
  • CORS: Restrictive cross-origin resource sharing policies
  • Audit Logging: Comprehensive audit trails for all data access and modifications
  • Infrastructure: Google Cloud Platform with SOC 2 Type II compliance
  • Monitoring: Real-time security monitoring and intrusion detection

While we implement robust security measures, no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

MHRAS is not intended for use with patients under 13 years of age without appropriate parental or guardian consent as required by the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). For patients aged 13-17, additional safeguards apply under state laws and HIPAA. Users must ensure appropriate consent is obtained before processing data for minors.

9. International Data Transfers

MHRAS is operated from the United States. If you access the System from outside the United States, your data will be transferred to and processed in the United States. By using the System, you consent to such transfers. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for international transfers through Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or other approved mechanisms as required by GDPR.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via email to registered users and through a prominent notice on the System at least 30 days before the changes take effect. Your continued use of the System after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
